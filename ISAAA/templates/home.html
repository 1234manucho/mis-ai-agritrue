Our Story

AgriTrue was born from a simple belief: every farmer deserves accurate, actionable information. In a world of agricultural misinformation, our mission is to restore trust and empower communities.

Our journey began at the 2025 AfriCenter hackathon, where we developed AI-powered tools to combat fake fertilizers and misleading data. We stand apart by making our technology inclusive and accessible to all farmers, regardless of connectivity.

We envision a future where farmers make data-driven decisions that boost productivity and nurture sustainable growth. With every insight we deliver, we're planting seeds of confidence for a more resilient agricultural future.

How it works

Step 1: Register or Sign In

Create a free AgriTrue account—choose web, mobile, or USSD. Just share your farm details, and you’re ready to start.

Step 2: Set Your Region

Tell AgriTrue where you farm. We tailor soil, weather, and advice based on your zone.

Step 3: Upload or Capture

Snap photos of your crops or input farm activity. Alternatively, input growing parameters manually or flag dubious claims.

Step 4: Analyze with Smart Tools

Leverage ML Analyzer for real-time insights. Or use 'Know Your Land' for soil type, fertility, and weather-informed recommendations. Plus, Farm Truth generates visuals to help you understand progress.

Step 5: Get Actionable Insights

Receive easy-to-understand guidance—crop inputs, pest alerts, irrigation scheduling, localized weather, and yield optimizations.

Step 6: Monitor & Improve

Track your farm’s growth with dashboards and progress trends. Learn what works and adjust dynamically.
