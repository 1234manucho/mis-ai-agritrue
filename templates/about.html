Empowering farmers through truth, technology and community.

Our foundation

A clear purpose guides every AgriTrue tool.

Our mission, vision and values define how we build, communicate and serve agricultural communities.

Our Mission

To combat agricultural misinformation by providing a reliable, community-driven and AI-assisted platform where farmers can verify information, share practical knowledge and make better-informed decisions.

Our Vision

To build a global community of informed and resilient farmers equipped with accessible knowledge and practical technology that strengthens farms, ecosystems and food systems.

Our Values

Integrity: Upholding truth and responsibility.

Community: Building supportive knowledge networks.

Innovation: Applying technology to real farming needs.

Sustainability: Supporting long-term agricultural resilience.

A community built on trust

Shared knowledge becomes stronger when it can be questioned and verified.

AgriTrue was created around a simple idea: farmers deserve agricultural information that is understandable, accessible and open to verification.

The platform connects practical farmer experience with digital tools, community participation and responsible AI-assisted guidance. Community Notes allow users to share observations and discuss claims, while AgriTrue tools help users decide when professional inspection or testing is necessary.

Local experience

Farmers contribute observations grounded in real agricultural conditions.

Responsible verification

Important decisions are linked back to experts, tests and trusted institutions.

Responsible agricultural technology

AI-assisted tools designed to support—not replace—professional judgement.

AgriTrue combines image screening, document review, agricultural question answering and community knowledge to help users understand a situation more clearly.

Digital results are presented as guidance rather than certainty. The platform encourages users to consult agronomists, veterinarians, extension officers, laboratories and issuing institutions whenever a decision is urgent, high-risk or uncertain.

Visual screening

Review visible crop or livestock symptoms using clear uploaded images.

Document screening

Identify visible concerns in farm documents while preserving the need for official verification.

How we work

Four principles guide our approach.

01

Be useful

Build tools around real farmer questions and workflows.

02

Be transparent

Explain limitations and avoid presenting screening as certainty.

03

Be accessible

Design for mobile users, voice access and varying connectivity.

04

Be accountable

Encourage verification through experts, testing and institutions.
