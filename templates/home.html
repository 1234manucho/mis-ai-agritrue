Our story

Technology should make trusted agricultural information easier to reach.

AgriTrue grew from a simple belief: farmers should not have to choose between inaccessible expert information and unverified advice. The platform combines practical digital tools with community participation to support more informed decisions.

From visual screening and land information to USSD, voice assistance and community notes, AgriTrue is designed to remain useful across different devices, connectivity levels and farming contexts.

Inclusive access

Mobile-first layouts, voice support and USSD-inspired workflows.

Responsible assistance

Clear reminders when professional inspection or testing is necessary.

How it works

Move from observation to an informed next step.

AgriTrue supports the process without pretending that digital screening replaces local professional expertise.

1

Capture or describe

Photograph visible symptoms, upload a document, select your county or write the agricultural question.

2

Review the guidance

Read the likely concerns, visible indicators, practical actions and limitations of the result.

3

Verify and act

Escalate urgent or uncertain cases to an agronomist, extension officer, veterinarian, laboratory or issuing institution.

The value of verification

A wrong recommendation can cost more than the input itself.

AgriTrue encourages farmers to verify claims before committing money, applying products or delaying professional intervention.

The risk
